İplik bulmacası

İpliği doğru sırayla çek, düğümleri çöz, her seviyede yeni bir kilim doku.

Yakında Google Play ve App Store'da.

Nasıl oynanır

  • İpliğe dokun: önü açıksa kayarak çıkar ve yumak olup tezgâha gider.
  • Önünde başka bir iplik varsa çıkamaz ve bir can gider.
  • Düğümlerde iki iplik üst üste geçer: önce üstteki çıkar.
  • Bağlı iki iplik birlikte çıkar; mühürlü iplik, aynı işaretli anahtar ipliği çekilmeden çıkamaz.
  • Örtülü ipliğe değen bir ipliği çekince örtü kalkar; makara ipliğin yolunu döndürür.
  • Çektiğin iplikler tezgâhta ilmek ilmek dokunur. Seviye bitince kilimin galerine girer.
  • Uşak'tan Hakkari'ye sekiz kilim bölgesi, 400 seviye: yolculuk haritasında her bölgenin kendi boyaları ve kilimi var. Ardından usta tezgâhında seviyeler bitmez.
  • Her gün yeni bir kilim; istersen akşamları kısa bir hatırlatma gelir. Oyun 11 dilde.

Dokuma

Thread puzzle

Pull threads in the right order, untie knots and weave a kilim every level.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

How to play

  • Tap a thread: if its way is clear, it slides out and flies to the loom as a ball of yarn.
  • If another thread is in the way, it can't move and you lose a life.
  • At knots, two threads cross over and under: the top one comes out first.
  • Tied threads come out together; a sealed thread waits until the key thread with the same mark is pulled.
  • Pull a thread touching a cover and the cover lifts; a pulley turns a thread's path.
  • The threads you pull are woven stitch by stitch on the loom. Finish the level and your kilim joins your gallery.
  • Eight kilim regions from Uşak to Hakkari, 400 levels: on the journey map each region has its own dyes and its own kilim. After that, the levels on the master loom never run out.
  • A new kilim every day, with an optional short reminder in the evening. The game is in 11 languages.

Support

Found a problem or have an idea? Write to mokur12345@gmail.com. Telling me your phone model helps me look into it faster.

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it can't be moved to another phone.
When do ads appear?
Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one (for a life, scissors or a comb). Ads between levels start after level 11 and half an hour of total play, with at least 90 seconds between them.
How do I change my ad consent choice?
In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: in the game, Settings → "Ad privacy options".
Can I play offline?
Yes. Only ads need a connection; without one, rewarded ads can't open.
